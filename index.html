A note on OMNIA

Curiosity has no borders.

OMNIA is an independent publication by Adeeb Sherif — a growing collection of essays, observations and questions about the world around us.

It begins with a simple belief: meaningful insight often appears at the intersection of different fields. A scientific idea can change how we see society. A philosophical question can reshape how we approach technology. One perspective is rarely the whole picture.

This is a space to explore those intersections, thoughtfully and without disciplinary boundaries.
